About the System

The School Lost and Found Management System helps students and staff report lost belongings, match them with found items, and safely track return processes through a simple online portal.

Report

Submit a lost item or found item with descriptions, locations, and photos.

Search

Browse by category, keyword, and location to quickly locate matches.

Recover

File claims and let administrators verify item ownership before return.
